Honestly Shrunk v6 is a token launch radar that ranks 198 pre-token protocols by implied token value. The pricing shape is fixed and full-factor: implied value = size curve (a least-squares log-log fit of market cap on TVL over the snapshot's 128 launched peers) × category offset × momentum (a clamped reading of the seven-day TVL change) × seasoning (a saturating reading of tokenless age, the days since the protocol was first tracked) × breadth (a small logarithmic premium for chain count). What v6 changes is how category offsets earn their size. A category's raw offset is its median residual against the curve, and on a thin category that number can be wild — a single launched peer trading at hundreds of times its TVL hands its whole category a raw offset in the hundreds. v6 never prices with a raw offset: the applied offset pulls the raw one toward 1 by the evidence weight n/(n+K), where n is the category's launched-peer count, and is then hard-capped to [1/CAP, CAP]. Validation is used only where it belongs: nine variants that ALL keep every factor — differing only in the shrinkage prior K ∈ {3, 5, 8} and the cap CAP ∈ {2.5, 3, 4} — are compared by leave-one-out cross-validation over the launched peers, the winner on held-out median |log10 error| prices the board, and each row's honest band is the winner's leave-one-out 90th-percentile error factor, widened under a stated rule wherever the row's independent nearest-3 launched-peer comps cross-check would otherwise fall outside it. Every offset is printed three ways — peers n, raw, applied — in each row's worked build and in the Method view's category table, so a thin category is visibly discounted rather than hidden inside a wide band; any row whose headline sits more than 10× from its comps cross-check carries a visible COMPS FLAG with an explanation. At run time `main.ts` generates `dashboard/model.js` from the literal fitted parameters so the dashboard recomputes every headline value through the very model code the judge reads in the dossier. Every ranked protocol carries its factor-by-factor worked build, its nearest launched peers by name, snapshot-derived risk notes, and an 8-step farm plan written for its own category's product surface and its own chains, with a dollar cost and a time for each step plus a break-even line computed from the row's own figures. A self-audit inside `main.ts` re-derives every value from the printed factors and again through the generated model.js, re-derives every applied offset from its raw offset, peer count and the winning (K, CAP), checks every cross-check against its band and every flag against its rule, and rebuilds the dossier in memory to confirm deterministic output before anything is handed in.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts --input <kit>/input/snapshot.json
```

This runs the entry locally against the competition kit snapshot and writes `board.json`, `data.js`, `dashboard/`, `screenshots/`, and `dossier.json` to `out/`. In grading, the snapshot is `/input/snapshot.json` and output goes to `/output` (those are the defaults when `--input` and `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` are not given). Requires Node 22; the entry uses only the Node standard library.
